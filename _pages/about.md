I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where my research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. I am advised by Professor Junxian He.

I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024, where I was supervised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

My complete publication list is also collected on the [Publications page]({{ base_path }}/publications/), and a curriculum vitae is available on the [CV page]({{ base_path }}/cv/).

## Skills and Research Interests

- Natural Language Processing
- Machine Learning
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
